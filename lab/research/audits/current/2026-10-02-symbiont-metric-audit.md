# Current metric audit: Symbiont agency and readiness

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Scope:** Current scientific metric vocabulary and its operational use for causal agency, causal revision, communication characterization, and individual readiness. This is a methodological audit and recommendation, not an amendment to a protocol, gate, threshold, or the scientific programme.
**Source revision inspected:** `4ce9e18f07df`.  
**Status:** Proposed for scientific owner review; no protocol change or experiment authorization.

## Question

Which measurements best distinguish the capabilities that currently condition individual readiness, without collapsing distinct failure modes into a score?

## Finding

The programme already rejects a universal “intelligence score” and lists capability-specific measures in `docs/methodology/research-programme.md` §27. Its readiness dimensions in §22, however, are mostly qualitative. Existing agency studies show that paired, condition-specific measures are more diagnostic than a single success rate. They also expose a limit in the retained run artifacts: the three result manifests inspected below all record `software.dirty = true`. Their reported outcomes are useful for locating metric strengths and weaknesses, but the archived artifacts alone do not establish clean-worktree reproducibility for those runs.

## Evidence inspected

### Temporal causal attribution

- Protocol: `experiments/embodiment/temporal-causality-challenge/experiment.toml`.
- Result: `experiments/embodiment/temporal-causality-challenge/results.json`.
- Current implementation: `src/symbiont_lab/studies/embodiment/temporal_causality_challenge.py`.
- The protocol preregisters detection rates for genuine effects at delay 0, 1, 3, and variable delay, an immediate external false-positive rate, and deterministic replay.
- The recorded results are: delay-0 detection `1.0`; delay-1 and delay-3 detection `0.0`; immediate external false-positive `1.0`; `h1_supported = false`.
- In the current runner, each “detection rate” is the fraction of seeds whose confidence meets `AGENCY_THRESHOLD`; it is not a threshold-independent discrimination statistic. This distinction must be retained when comparing results across a different threshold or runner version.

### Hidden common cause

- Result: `experiments/embodiment/hidden-common-cause/results.json`.
- Current implementation: `src/symbiont_lab/studies/embodiment/hidden_common_cause.py`.
- The implementation exposes true-positive rate and false-positive rates separately for confounded and independent conditions. The saved result reports true-positive `1.0`, confounded false-positive `1.0`, independent false-positive `0.0`, and `h1_supported = false`.
- A pooled false-positive rate would hide the critical failure in the confounded condition; the negative-control condition is not a substitute for the adversarial confound condition.

### Causal revision sequence

- Protocol: `experiments/embodiment/causal-revision-sequence/experiment.toml`.
- Result: `experiments/embodiment/causal-revision-sequence/results.json`.
- Current implementation: `src/symbiont_lab/studies/embodiment/causal_revision_sequence.py`.
- The implementation defines revision as `revision_delta > 0` and separately records whether the mapping changed. The result reports permutation mapping-change rate `1.0` but permutation revision rate `0.0`; transplant mapping-change rate `1.0` but transplant revision rate `0.1`; break revision rate `0.0`; sham specificity `1.0`; and replay deterministic `true`.
- These are distinct observables. A changed mapping cannot be used as a proxy for causal-model revision, and a revision count without a sham comparison cannot establish selective updating.

### Provenance limitation

All three saved manifests report `software.dirty = true`; the temporal-causality artifact records Git SHA `64a24d0b...`, while the other two record `215145d8...`. These differ from the inspected source revision. Therefore, this audit uses the archived outcomes as bounded diagnostic evidence and inspects current runner definitions separately; it does not assert that those results were generated by the exact clean source tree inspected here. No rerun was performed.

### Communication metric availability

- Study protocol and report: `experiments/learning/structured-communication-characterization/experiment.toml` and `research/studies/communication/structured-communication-characterization-v1/README.md`.
- Result artifact: `experiments/learning/structured-communication-characterization/results.json`.
- Current implementation: `src/symbiont_lab/studies/learning/structured_communication_characterization.py`.
- The no-signal control correctly records zero messages and zero unique symbols/sequences. However, the saved rows report `symbol_reuse_rate = 1.0` and `sequence_reuse_rate = 1.0`, because the implementation divides by `max(1, event_count)` and calculates one minus unique/count. With no events, these values mean “maximal reuse” numerically even though reuse is undefined. This conflicts with the study's own no-message interpretation and is a metric semantic defect, not evidence of communication.
- The artifact also provides event and unique counts needed to identify this empty case. No protocol or result was changed. A versioned future metric definition should represent zero-denominator rates as unavailable (for example `null`) with the denominator retained; a zero rate should mean observed events with no repeats. Any migration must preserve the original artifact and classifier history.

## Recommended metric portfolio

Do not create a cross-capability scalar. The canonical programme §27 already points to causal agency, body acquisition, genetics, OOD, and social capability families. For each preregistered condition and seed, retain the relevant measures as separate dimensions:

1. **Causal discrimination:** true-positive rate for genuine effects and false-positive rate separately for independent, confounded, yoked-external, and sham conditions where applicable. Always publish numerators, denominators, and the condition-specific rates; do not pool conditions that represent different rival explanations.
2. **Confidence behavior:** retain the per-seed continuous confidence distributions for each condition alongside the preregistered thresholded decision rate. Call confidence “calibrated” only if a separate calibration analysis, with an explicit evaluator-side outcome definition and adequate independent cases, is specified and validated. Current confidence values and one operating threshold alone do not demonstrate calibration.
3. **Selective causal revision:** report belief/model revision and structural mapping change separately, then compare real contradiction/intervention conditions against sham. Include no-revision counts and latency censoring/horizon rules; do not compute latency only over successful revisers without showing the non-reviser fraction.
4. **Temporal robustness:** report detection and false-positive rates by delay class, not only a pooled delayed-attribution result. Variable-delay performance must remain distinct from fixed-delay performance.
5. **Readiness continuity and reacclimation:** use identity/state continuity checks as integrity outcomes; measure body-specific uncertainty change and reacquisition/recovery separately across the preregistered A-B-A phases. A single return advantage or final-state match cannot substitute for continuity and reacclimation evidence.
6. **Evidence integrity:** report clean/dirty source state, exact commit, protocol digest/version, seeds, horizons, replay result, and missing/censored cases as an independent validity layer, never as points in capability performance.
7. **Communication and other event-derived rates:** retain raw event/eligibility denominators, unique-event counts, silence/opportunity counts, and condition-specific utility/control contrasts. Mark rates with zero eligible events as unavailable rather than assigning a favorable or adverse numeric rate. Keep prediction gain, code structure, held-out utility, and productivity as separate claims; evidence for one does not establish the others.
8. **Genetic, social, and OOD measures:** report genotype-expression consistency alongside phenotype divergence and inherited-state isolation; social signal use separately from dependency, acquisition, source calibration, and persistence; and OOD degradation separately from uncertainty response, recovery, stale-belief persistence, and transfer. These are programme-listed metric families, not evidence that every construct is currently operationalized or validated.

For summary across seeds, show per-seed outcomes and a distribution/uncertainty interval appropriate to the independent unit. The seed is the unit only if the protocol's execution structure supports treating seeds as independent; repeated observations within one run must not be counted as independent organisms. Do not recommend ROC/AUC or calibration summaries until the independent sampling unit and case count support them.

## Decision requested

**Recommended owner decision:** accept this portfolio as a non-binding metric-design direction for future readiness protocols, then authorize a separate, versioned protocol/design update to operationalize definitions, denominators, censoring, analysis units, uncertainty intervals, and acceptance thresholds. Do not retrofit these definitions into frozen results or change current thresholds based on the outcomes summarized here.

Until that decision, §22/§27 remain unchanged, no experiment is authorized by this audit, and all recorded results retain their existing bounded status. The dirty manifests should be treated as a reproducibility/provenance limitation and resolved through a separately authorized clean rerun or provenance audit, not by silently upgrading or discarding the reported outcomes.
