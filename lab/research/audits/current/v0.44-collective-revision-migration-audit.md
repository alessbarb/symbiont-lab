## 5. Invariants Extracted
- **Source-specific empirical reliability:** The organism evaluates source claims based on localized trust, not global authority.
- **Non-majoritarian collective revision:** The aggregate is trust-weighted, so 51% low-trust agreement does not override a high-trust source.
- **Preservation of dissent:** Outliers are not discarded; they are explicitly flagged as dissenting claims for historical/contextual preservation.
- **Signed/provenance-bearing knowledge exchange:** Capsules must be verified cryptographically before their claims are considered.

## 6. Algorithmic Ideas Extracted
- **Dissent standard deviation thresholding:** Using weighted mean and weighted variance to dynamically compute `dissent_threshold` distances.
- **Trust flooring:** Zero-reliability sources are given a minimal visibility floor rather than absolute 0.0 weight so their claims aren't mathematically annihilated.

## 7. Explicitly Rejected Designs
- **CLI-only aggregation:** The `capsule aggregate` command is rejected because aggregation must happen organically inside the organism's cognitive loop, not outside.
- **Scalar trust as truth probability:** `SourceTrustModel` must be evolved into `SourceEvidenceModel` (multidimensional context).
- **Direct MotorCompetence prior:** The aggregate cannot directly mutate causal factual state or `MotorCompetence`. It must act strictly as a non-authoritative hypothesis.
- **Truth by consensus:** "Majority-as-truth" is strictly rejected. A supported hypothesis must survive real empirical action.
