# Cultural Foundation v1 — audit and outcome

Date: 2026-09-17  
Study: `learning.cultural-foundation`  
Seeds: `101, 127, 149`  
Protocol version: 1

## Causal finding

Before this phase there was no social claim contract or separate received-
evidence ledger. Private experience and private model state therefore remain
unmodified; the new capability is a bounded social layer around native tokens.

## Preregistered gates

All seven gates passed for every seed:

- C1: A -> B -> C -> D preserved claim roots, parents and depth;
- C2: ten holders/copies retained one independent root;
- C3: a new local evidence id added one root without rewriting the received
  genealogy;
- C4: support and contradiction remained separate assessments;
- C5: the authorized social protocol reached its declared useful state in 4
  ticks/cost 3 versus 12 ticks/cost 12 for the solitary protocol;
- C6: repeated rumor copies did not create independent evidence;
- C7: a later ledger acquired the claim after the discoverer was removed.

All replay checks were deterministic (`3/3`).

## Limits

The closure is limited to bounded claims, local in-memory transport, and the
declared study. C5 is a mechanism-level utility comparison: the laboratory
controls delivery. It is not evidence that cooperation, truth detection, or a
sharing policy emerged autonomously. No weights, adapters, corpus, host data,
or executable payloads cross the channel. Cumulative culture remains a future
study.
