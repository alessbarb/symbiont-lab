## Adversarial findings

### Evaluator-to-organism separation

The integrated runtime provides only deterministic habitat surfaces, authorized
contact pairs, budgets and lifecycle orchestration. It does not pass ground
truth, latent labels, evaluator metrics, meanings or Observatory aggregates to
organism decisions. Cultural and sequence decisions remain in the organism
runtime. No new scaffold was introduced by the final Observatory fix.

### Inheritance and ownership

The newborn integration test verifies fresh experience, social claims and
sequence exposures while preserving only the inherited genome/capacity. Private
model registries and acquired cultural state remain per organism. Telemetry is
observational and is not inherited as knowledge.

### Replay and randomness

Checkpointing is restricted to end-of-tick boundaries and restores population
identity, lifecycle state, ledgers, transport state and bounded history. The
smoke replay is deterministic for all three registered seeds. No global random
state was introduced by the integration path.

### Transport-capacity fix

The runtime catches only the exact existing capacity-exhaustion message. Other
`ValueError` instances remain fail-closed. It does not retry, substitute a
message, select another receiver or alter organism RNG. Transport capacity is a
causal habitat constraint and is represented in the integrated configuration,
checkpoint and fingerprint rather than treated as telemetry-only.

### Observatory truthfulness

The Observatory consumes local snapshots/streams and does not mutate runtime
state. Population edges are sourced from exported events; no historical edge is
fabricated from a snapshot lacking telemetry. Legacy and truncated states are
shown with explicit empty/truncation semantics. Organism-facing state remains
separate from evaluator analysis; opaque messages are not translated.

## Remaining limitations (accepted P2/P3)

- The 10,000-tick stress result is seed `101` only; it is not a population-wide
  statistical ecology claim.
- The integrated technical smoke does not by itself establish emergent
  multigenerational phenomena or new scientific claims.
- No real networking, peer discovery, language, open-ended evolution or
  cultural selection is in scope.
- Browser QA is local Chrome QA, not CI/browser-matrix coverage.
- The known PyTorch warning remains non-fatal and unrelated to integration.
