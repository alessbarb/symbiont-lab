## Supported interpretation

Under this protocol, increasing organism-requested replay compute from the
constitutional minimum (2 epochs / 12 steps) to the L7.2 maximum
(8 epochs / 48 steps) extracts substantially more held-out predictive utility
from exactly the same causal experience, corpus, tokenizer, model seed,
architecture, objective and holdout.

This supports the narrower claim that extra compute can improve learning from
fixed experience without adding new world observations.

## Important limitation

All three treatment seeds had `replay_pressure = 1.0`.

Therefore this experiment does **not** validate the adaptive pressure function
across its range. It validates only the endpoint contrast:

```text
minimum replay budget  vs  maximum replay budget
```

It does not yet show that intermediate pressures receive an efficient or
monotonic amount of compute, nor that contradiction pressure and experience
pressure are calibrated relative to one another.

No post-hoc change to the L7.2 pressure function is justified by this result.

## Next falsification gate

L7.4 should preregister a replay-pressure dose-response study with matched
experience and pressure levels spanning the interior of [0, 1].

The test should ask whether predictive utility improves monotonically or shows a
useful saturation curve as replay work rises, while reporting compute cost.

A suitable design compares fixed matched budgets corresponding to pressures such
as 0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0 while preserving the exact same causal corpus,
model seed and held-out split within each seed.

The evaluator remains observational only.
