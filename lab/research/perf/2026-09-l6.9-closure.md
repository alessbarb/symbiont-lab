# L6.9 Performance Closure — 2026-09-22

Status: closed for transparent runtime optimization.

## Final decision

L6.9 is closed because the remaining dominant CPU costs are now primarily
learning work rather than obvious administrative waste.

The final 1,000-tick cognitive profile after L6.9.10 reported:

- 17,078,603 total function calls;
- 5.828 s profiler wall time;
- `OrganismRuntime.tick`: 6.038 s cumulative;
- `CognitiveBridge.tick`: 2.462 s cumulative;
- `ShadowPrediction.observe`: 0.469 s cumulative;
- `huber_loss`: 0.201 s cumulative;
- `predictive_gain`: 0.116 s cumulative;
- `_prune_shadow_predictions`: only 0.041 s cumulative.

This confirms that the final shadow-pruning dirty fast path removed the last
large known redundant full-pool scan.

## Important interpretation

The 512-tick embodied runs at the end of the optimization session are
continuations of one developing organism. Their wall times are therefore not a
clean A/B performance benchmark because graph, sensorimotor state, private
models, schema and accumulated experience all evolve between runs.

Use `scripts/bench_organism_tick.py`,
`scripts/profile_cognitive_hotpath.py`, and
`scripts/bench_cognitive_scaling.py` for controlled performance comparisons.

## What remains intentionally unoptimized

The following costs are preserved unless a later equivalence study proves a
strictly transparent implementation improvement:

- shadow-prediction evidence updates;
- Huber prediction-loss computation;
- predictive-gain calculation;
- Hebbian/Oja plasticity;
- sensory transduction and downstream utility;
- genuine sensorimotor learning.

Reducing how often these mechanisms learn merely to improve throughput would
change the experiment rather than optimize its implementation.

## Transition to L7

Recovered compute is now redirected toward sample-efficient autonomous learning.

The first L7 step reuses the organism's existing causal `ExperienceLedger`
and moves replay/training demand from a fixed Physics3D schedule into the
organism itself. The host remains a compute substrate and independent promotion
authority, not a teacher or curriculum scheduler.
