# K3 concept ceiling characterization report

Date: 2026-09-22  
Control: `192 nodes / 1536 edges / 32 concepts`  
Seeds: 10 paired seeds  
Protocol: `K3`, synthetic concept-demand screen

## Results

| max_concepts | mean prediction error | mean CPU us/tick | concepts used | failures |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8   | 0.033592 | 1971.4 | 8   | 0 |
| 16  | 0.030970 | 1873.9 | 16  | 0 |
| 24  | 0.028397 | 1893.0 | 24  | 0 |
| 32  | 0.025876 | 1868.6 | 32  | 0 |
| 48  | 0.021003 | 2015.1 | 48  | 0 |
| 64  | 0.016368 | 1941.1 | 64  | 0 |
| 96  | 0.007858 | 1862.9 | 96  | 0 |
| 128 | 0.000378 | 1926.7 | 128 | 0 |

The synthetic demand is capacity-sensitive and improves throughout the tested
range. There is no observed plateau at 32 or 64 concepts. This is evidence
that `max_concepts=32` can be a ceiling for this probe, not evidence that the
organism benefits from 128 concepts in production: the probe has no real
concept discovery, consolidation or transfer path.

## Decision

- Do not promote a larger production concept limit.
- Mark `32` as **synthetically capacity-limiting** and retain it as the
  canonical control for subsequent embodied tests.
- Candidate follow-up values are 64 and 96; 128 is a high-cost upper probe,
  not a recommendation.
- Before changing production, repeat K3 with real concept formation,
  pruning/reuse and Physics3D confirmation.
