# Digital Physiology and the Metabolism of Thought

To construct a mind is to build an engine that burns energy to produce order. Within the Symbiont architecture, the act of processing information is fundamentally intertwined with the thermodynamics of survival; thought is never free. Every time the organism dares to sample a sensor, sparks a neural node into activation, or desperately clings to a fleeting memory, it irreversibly consumes finite, life-sustaining energy. As these internal reserves dwindle, the organism is forced into a grim calculus, shedding its higher cognitive functions to protect its physical core. If this delicate balance fails and the reserves drain to zero, the biological structure undergoes a catastrophic and permanent collapse. This document delineates the internal economy of the organism, tracing the mathematical thresholds that dictate its vital homeostasis and its eventual death.

The beating heart of this survival imperative is the `MetabolicLedger`. This precise accounting mechanism ensures the organism balances its existence across four uncompromising resources. The first is **Observation**, the baseline energy tax levied every time the organism reaches out to sample the chaotic currents of the external world. The second is **Cognition**, the immense metabolic friction generated when the internal graph is activated and the structural weights of the mind are altered through learning. The third, **Persistence**, demands energy to forge short-term electrical flickers into durable, consolidated memory. Finally, there is the **Maintenance** cost—the inescapable, ambient burn required to keep the cellular structure alive, a persistent drain levied regardless of whether the organism is thinking, acting, or resting.

The organism’s perception of its own suffering—its physiological pressure—is not abstracted as a vague sensation, but is instead crystallized into a merciless mathematical ratio. For every resource maintained by the ledger, the organism constantly evaluates its current standing: the ratio of the available reserve divided by the absolute biological capacity. The system’s overall stress is defined by its most critical vulnerability, determined by locating the minimum ratio across all monitored resources.

Driven by this vital metric, the `HomeostaticController` enforces a cascade of survival states. When the organism is thriving, with its critical ratio sitting comfortably above 0.5, it remains in a Normal state, operating with 100% cognitive and physical fluidity. As energy wanes and the ratio dips into the Elevated band (between 0.2 and 0.5), a subtle lethargy takes hold; overall activity is systematically scaled down to 90%, damping the organism's interactions to preserve what remains. If starvation deepens and the ratio plunges below 0.2, the organism enters a Severe physiological crisis. Its activity plummets to 75%, but more profoundly, the architecture intervenes to save the body at the expense of the mind. At this severe threshold, cognitive plasticity is entirely disabled. The organism forcefully stops learning, abandoning its ability to adapt to the future so it might simply survive the present. Should the ratio slip below absolute zero, the organism crashes into an Unrecoverable state of multi-organ failure.

To stave off this slow suffocation, the organism must constantly battle the accumulation of its own history. If it retained every memory indefinitely, the compounded maintenance cost of its massive cognitive graph would rapidly consume its energy budget. To survive its own intelligence, the organism employs a biological `DegradationQueue`. Knowledge is subjected to a ruthless temporal lifecycle. A structure begins as ACTIVE, but if left unreinforced for 16 consecutive ticks, it begins to rot, transitioning into an AGING state. Eight ticks later, the memory decays into mere WASTE. Finally, an aging sweep pushes it into the EXCRETED state. At this terminal boundary, the structural memory is violently and irreversibly torn from the graph, dissolving the neural pathways and instantly freeing the maintenance budget that the memory had been leeching.

Despite all adaptations, death remains the ultimate, non-negotiable boundary. When the `PhysiologyController` detects terminal failure, it shifts the system to `VitalState.DEAD`. This transition is irreversible. The moment it occurs, the `OrganismRuntime.tick()` raises a fatal `OrganismDeadError`, physically paralyzing any attempt at further computation. In the same atomic instant, the system purges the corpse from the environment. The `SharedHabitat.release()` and `SocialHabitat.release()` protocols are triggered, abruptly vacating the physical coordinates and severing all social tethers the organism held. Finally, the entity's lineage within the `HabitatBirthAuthority` is permanently sealed. Because these acts of erasure are executed atomically at the lowest runtime layer, the mathematical integrity of the simulation is absolute. An organism cannot cheat its own biology, nor can a starved, phantom mind continue to learn in the dark.
