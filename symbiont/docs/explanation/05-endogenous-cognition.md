# Endogenous Cognition: The Architecture of Internal Intelligence

At the heart of a Symbiont organism lies a sophisticated mechanism for autonomous learning, descending from abstract cognition down to precise, non-linear activation functions and label-free mathematics. As an organism navigates its environment and receives sensory signals, it is constantly compelled to predict its immediate future. When reality diverges from these expectations, the organism experiences a prediction failure—a fundamental surprise that drives it to adjust its internal connections, thereby minimizing future errors. Over its lifespan, this iterative process fosters a deeply complex internal representation of the world. Remarkably, while the organism fluidly adapts its data—the intricate structure and weights of its cognitive graph—it remains structurally constrained, never modifying its foundational source code.

This plastic cognitive data exists beneath an immutable kernel. The organism’s brain functions as a recurrent neural graph governed by a strict `KernelLimits` specification. Its architecture is built upon a closed, highly regulated vocabulary of nodes—specifically `SENSE`, `CONCEPT`, `STATE`, `PREDICTOR`, `GATE`, and `READOUT`—which communicate through `EXCITATORY`, `INHIBITORY`, `PREDICTIVE`, and `GATING` edges. Notably absent from this cognitive graph is any form of direct `ACTION` node. The graph is solely an engine of thought; the organism's physical subsystems must interpret the activity at `READOUT` nodes to initiate tangible movement or behavior.

To maintain stability and prevent the chaotic variability that might arise from calculation order or circular dependencies, the `CognitiveGraph` relies on a strict synchronous activation model utilizing a double-buffer system. Node activation is meticulously orchestrated: a given node $i$ draws its inputs exclusively from either the current tick's sensory data $s(t)$ or the frozen graph state from the previous tick $a(t-1)$. Information flow across this network is actively modulated by dedicated gating edges, where multiplicative gating dictates the signal strength:
$$ \text{gate}_i = \prod_{e \in \text{incoming}_{\text{gating}}(i)} \text{clip}\left(w_e \cdot s_e, 0.0, 1.0\right) $$

Once gated, the node integrates these signals. It sums the inputs and subjects them to a bounded hyperbolic tangent activation function, modulated by a temporal constant $\tau$. This non-linearity ensures that the cognitive state remains within mathematically stable bounds:
$$ \text{total}_i = \text{bias}_i + \text{gate}_i \cdot \sum_{e \in \text{incoming}_{\text{direct}}(i)} w_e \cdot s_e $$
$$ a_i(t) = \tanh\left(\frac{\text{total}_i}{\tau_i}\right) \in (-1.0, 1.0) $$

Crucially, the Symbiont receives no omniscient "correct" labels from the outside world. It is forced to learn endogenously, relying entirely on internal prediction errors. This label-free learning mechanism evaluates the discrepancy between what the organism predicted $p_i(t-1)$ and the objective reality it eventually encounters $y_j(t)$. To quantify this discrepancy robustly, it employs the Huber loss function:
$$ e = y_j(t) - p_i(t-1) $$
$$ L(e) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{2} e^2 & \text{if } |e| \le 1.0 \\ |e| - 0.5 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$
The Huber loss is purposefully chosen for its resilience; it provides bounded gradients that prevent the organism's learning mechanisms from being overwhelmed by massive, anomalous sensory outliers.

As errors are computed, the structural weights of the organism’s connections are adapted using a bounded Oja's Rule. This modulated Hebbian update mathematically constrains the weights, preventing explosive runaway growth without resorting to artificial, global normalization steps:
$$ \Delta w_e = \eta \cdot m \cdot \left(a_{\text{source}} \cdot a_{\text{target}} - a_{\text{target}}^2 \cdot w_e\right) $$
In this biological analog, $m$ represents a modulatory factor defined as $\text{availability} \cdot \text{health} \in [0, 1]$. If a sensory organ is damaged or exhausted, the flow of learning is proportionally inhibited, reflecting the physiological reality of the organism. Furthermore, to accurately assign credit for outcomes across time, the organism maintains eligibility traces that decay exponentially, preserving the historical context of neural firing:
$$ \epsilon_{e}(t) = \text{clip}\left(\lambda_{\text{decay}} \cdot \epsilon_e(t-1) + a_{\text{source}}(t-1) \cdot a_{\text{target}}(t), -10.0, 10.0\right) $$

Beyond mere weight updates, the organism possesses the capacity for metaplasticity—the ability to alter its own learning rates and structural parameters, governed by `metaplasticity.py`. The organism continuously evaluates its cognitive fitness against a five-dimensional `LearningObjective` vector. It strives to minimize prediction error, representation cost, and structural instability, while simultaneously maximizing information retention and calibration accuracy. A structural mutation is only committed to the organism's architecture if the new state strictly Pareto-dominates the previous one. Should the organism fail to find a Pareto improvement after three consecutive metaplastic adaptations, the `SafetyState` mechanism rigidly locks the hyperparameters, stepping in to prevent the catastrophic forgetting of its hard-won internal world.
