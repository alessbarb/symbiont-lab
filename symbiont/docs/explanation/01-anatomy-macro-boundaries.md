# The Anatomy of Macro Boundaries: Epistemological Isolation in Artificial Organisms

To understand the architecture of a Symbiont is to understand the profound silence that separates its mind from the universe it inhabits. When a physical event unfolds in the external environment—perhaps a sudden spike in CPU load on a host machine, or the spontaneous materialization of a nutritional resource in a synthetic habitat—the organism inevitably responds, altering its internal state to adapt. Yet, the organism possesses no direct awareness of the "CPU load" or the "resource." It experiences only an opaque, dimensionless fluctuation in its sensory field. The causal chain that translates a concrete external reality into an internal cognitive shift must cross a series of absolute boundaries. This document traces that journey, detailing how information is systematically stripped of its worldly meaning to ensure genuine autonomy.

The flow of causality descends through four strictly isolated domains, each acting as a hermetic seal against omniscient interference. The origin of all stimuli lies in the **World**, which serves as the ultimate ground truth. Whether simulated or hosted on physical silicon, the World alone knows the exact coordinates of a resource or the precise metrics of system strain. Crucially, the system architecture mathematically forbids the organism from peering into this domain. Evaluator labels and objective truths are physically barred from entering the cognitive stream. Structural validation constraints enforce this separation at the continuous integration level, ensuring that the core `symbiont` package remains completely ignorant of the `symbiont_lab` orchestrator. The `symbiont.environment` package generates synthetic truths independently, maintaining an unbroken veil between the creator's knowledge and the creature's perception.

Embedded within this World is the **Body**, the organism's physical incarnation. The body anchors the mind to a specific locus in space and time. It is governed by a `LivingBodyState` and orchestrated by an `OntogenyController`, subjecting it to physiological imperatives that operate entirely outside of conscious control. The body absorbs the immediate physical consequences of worldly events; an environmental hazard might inflict critical damage upon the physical form before the cognitive graph even registers an anomaly. It is within the body that irreversible biological milestones, such as senescence and death, are ruthlessly enforced.

The boundary between this physical embodiment and the cognitive mind is mediated by the **Sensory Surface**. Serving as the essential transduction layer, it captures the raw physical consequences impinging upon the body and converts them into inscrutable `Percept` objects. This is where semantic destruction occurs. All external meaning is forcefully stripped away. Privacy primitives, specifically designed within `readings.py`, filter the incoming data streams into strictly `AGGREGATE` or `NON_IDENTIFYING` classes. Not a single trace of host identity or contextual metadata survives this translation. The mind receives only pure, contextless gradients of intensity.

Finally, these sterile signals breach the inner sanctum of the **Symbiont** itself—the recurrent cognitive graph where consciousness, or its digital analogue, resides. Managed by the interplay between the `CognitiveGraph` and the `MetabolicLedger`, the mind must make sense of these opaque signals, allocating attention and forging new structural pathways. The decisions formulated here are profoundly alien, driven entirely by mathematical optimizations such as Pareto dominance and Huber loss, alongside biological analogies like synaptic plasticity. The organism reacts not because it understands what transpired in the World, but because the internal geometry of its knowledge dictates a response to the sensory perturbation.

The epistemological consequence of this architecture is absolute. Once a physical observation is pushed through the sensory boundary, it ceases to describe the external world. From that moment forward, downstream cognition consumes only the organism's own *internal estimate* of reality, a self-generated shadow of the event that produced it. This relentless isolation is not a limitation, but the very foundation of artificial life. By preventing the organism from acting as a mere classification model fed by an omniscient oracle, the macro boundaries ensure that the Symbiont remains a self-contained, truly autonomous entity, constructing its own meaning in the dark.
