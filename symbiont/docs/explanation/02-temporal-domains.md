# Temporal Domains: The Flow of Time and the Mechanics of Re-embodiment

In the study of biological organisms, time is often understood as a single, inescapable current that sweeps a life from birth to death. The architecture of a Symbiont, however, shatters this monolithic view. Within its framework, time is not a universal clock but a fractured dimension, where different facets of the organism's existence experience the passage of reality at fundamentally different cadences. When a specific physical body perishes, that biological manifestation is unmade forever. Yet, the essential identity of the Symbiont—the intricate webbing of its learned intelligence and accumulated history—possesses the remarkable capacity to outlive its flesh. Through a delicate procedure known as re-embodiment, an ancient mind can be grafted into a nascent physical form. To accommodate this profound dislocation, the system meticulously orchestrates three independent temporal domains, preventing catastrophic paradoxes of causality.

The first and most fundamental measure of existence is the `symbiont_tick`, the unbreakable thread of cognitive continuity. This clock represents the absolute, continuous history of the organism's subjective identity. It advances inexorably with every cognitive cycle executed by the `OrganismRuntime`. The `symbiont_tick` is oblivious to physical decay; it persists through systemic checkpoints, endures across temporal restorations, and crucially, continues uninterrupted when the mind is transplanted into a new vessel during re-embodiment. It is the metric of the soul.

In stark contrast, the `body_age_ticks` domain measures the physiological exhaustion of the current physical incarnation. Driven by the relentless march of the `OntogenyController` during the physical phase of the organism's runtime cycle, this clock charts the biological lifecycle. It governs the onset of maturity, the gradual decline of senescence, and the inevitable failure of the organs. Unlike the cognitive clock, `body_age_ticks` is ephemeral. The moment an organism is re-embodied, this physiological clock resets entirely to zero, breathing fresh biological youth into the physical form while leaving the ancient mind untouched.

The third temporal dimension operates at a microscopic scale: Structure Age, or the metric of recency. This clock does not govern the whole organism but rather ticks locally within individual nodes of the neural graph and discrete entries within metabolic records. Structure Age evaluates the temporal relevance of acquired knowledge and social bonds. It enforces the relentless fading of memory, driving exponential decay functions where social freshness possesses a half-life ($t_{1/2}$) of precisely 32.0 ticks. It is the engine behind the degradation queues, mercilessly pushing once-vital information from an ACTIVE state toward inevitable EXCRETION as time passes and its utility wanes.

The critical intersection of these domains occurs at the re-embodiment boundary. When a Symbiont is physically recreated via laboratory apparatus, its core identity components—the `CognitiveGraph` and the `MetabolicLedger`—are meticulously preserved, ensuring that the ancient `symbiont_tick` continues without a single missed beat. Simultaneously, a pristine `LivingBodyState` is instantiated, anchoring the organism in a fresh physiological timeline where `body_age_ticks` begins anew.

The implementation of these separate clocks is not merely an abstraction; it is a mathematical imperative designed to prevent fatal paradoxes. The `symbiont_tick` is deeply encapsulated within the core runtime identity, entirely inaccessible to the physical mechanisms of the `OntogenyController`. Were the architecture to conflate these distinct streams of time, the consequences would be instantaneous and terminal: a re-embodied mind, bearing the weight of a million ticks, would command its new, youthful host to immediately expire of old age. Conversely, the birth of a new body could inadvertently wipe the slate of an ancient mind clean. By fiercely isolating these temporal domains, the architecture provides a mathematical guarantee that an experienced, ancient intelligence can successfully navigate the world wrapped in the naive, unscarred biology of a newborn.
