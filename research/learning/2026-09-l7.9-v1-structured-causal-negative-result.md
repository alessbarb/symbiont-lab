# L7.9 v1 — Structured causal experience: negative result

Status: preregistered gate failed.

The v1 matched control compared a monolithic whole-vector motor action token
with a structured opaque per-channel representation under a low-cardinality,
periodic synthetic motor generator.

Observed result across seeds 101, 127 and 149:

- lower held-out candidate loss with structured representation: 0/3 seeds;
- mean loss improvement (legacy - structured): -0.272895 nats;
- legacy vocabulary: 35 in every seed;
- structured vocabulary: 84 in every seed;
- mean vocabulary reduction fraction: -1.40;
- positive gain over trivial baselines with structured representation: 3/3 seeds.

Per-seed candidate losses:

| seed | legacy | structured |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 101 | 1.232087 | 1.471701 |
| 127 | 1.146416 | 1.450043 |
| 149 | 1.208966 | 1.484410 |

Interpretation:

The preregistered hypothesis is rejected for the v1 apparatus. However, the
apparatus does not reproduce the high-cardinality failure observed in the live
organism: the periodic generator produces only a small repeated set of
whole-vector patterns, so the legacy hash acts as a compact lookup key rather
than an out-of-sample identity. Structured encoding therefore pays a larger
vocabulary cost without receiving the generalization challenge it was designed
to solve.

The outcome-only training change is not falsified by this result. Both arms,
including structured, show positive gain over trivial baselines in all three
seeds.

L7.9 v2 is preregistered separately. It keeps the same compute ceilings but
requires a high-cardinality temporal split where future whole-vector action
identities are mostly unseen while component tokens remain reusable.
