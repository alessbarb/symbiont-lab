# Adversarial audit: Emergent Structured Communication v1

## Scope

This audit precedes interpretation of `learning.emergent-structured-communication`.
The former Proto-language study was not used as evidence: its fixed pair/holdout
design and privileged compositional predictor were removed from the active path.

## Checks

- Sequence construction is variable-length and uses opaque IDs only.
- `symbiont` chooses silence/message content from local state; the harness only
  supplies authorized contact opportunities.
- No factor names, semantic roles, position parser, grammar, target sequence,
  canonical code or evaluator utility enters the runtime path.
- Receiver grounding is exact and local; a sequence is not an observation and
  cannot become a Private SLM target automatically.
- Random and permutation conditions are evaluator controls, not autonomous
  treatment decisions.
- Costs, ledger bounds, checkpoint state and clone isolation remain enforced.
- Observatory remains read-only and exposes no human translation.

## Residual limitations

The baseline policy is deterministic and heuristic, not an emergent learned
communication policy. Contact topology and windows remain apparatus-controlled.
The initial receiver ledger learns whole-message associations, so productive
structure is an empirical question and is not guaranteed. A positive utility
result must not be called grammar or language without additional evidence.
