The probe's task result is invariant because K2 adds zero-weight delayed

screen, not evidence that the added edges are cognitively useful.

## Decision

- The current canonical `max_edges=1536` is not justified or rejected by K2.
- The synthetic cost-only screen would prefer the smallest tested value, 384,
  but that must not be promoted: useful structural connectivity was not tested.
- Keep `max_edges=1536` while a task-coupled connectivity probe is designed.
- Do not use K2 to claim an ideal edge density. The next iteration must use
  real candidate structural edges, causal task demand and consolidation, then
  confirm survivors in Physics3D.
