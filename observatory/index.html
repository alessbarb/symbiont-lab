Help & Guide

How to understand the visualization

Dual Perspectives: Phenotype vs. Self

The Individual view offers two epistemologically distinct perspectives:

Phenotype (Scientific Apparatus View) The external model: cellular membrane, stratified sensory receptors, plastic recurrent graph, and evidence-weighted belief constellations.
Self (BodySchema Engine) The organism's own internal representation: self-discovered sensory parts (health, confidence, cost, maturity) and learned cognitive regions with functional dependencies (co_acts_with, precedes).

Reading the Phenotype

Reactive Cellular Membrane

The boundary's color, tension, and halo reflect homeostatic equilibrium:

Mint Glow (Adaptive) Healthy homeostatic equilibrium and intact synaptic topology.
Coral / Stress Aura (Alert) Environmental drift (regime shift) or elevated contradictory evidence.
Amber Glow (Recovering) Topological recovery or re-acclimation phase after host disruption.
Desaturated / Grey (Frozen) Safety freeze engaged due to consecutive prediction error limits.

Sensory Receptors (Left Margin)

Vetted, read-only OS capabilities organized by developmental tiers:

Active (Cyan Solid + Pulse) Routinely attended channel; actively sampled during the current tick cycle.
Probing (Amber Dashed Halo) Rotating exploratory budget investigating whether this sensor provides signal.
Dormant (Muted Dashed Ring) Quiescent channel conserving metabolic and computational budget.
Utility Gauge & Labels Outer green arc shows learned utility; ladder labels identify the signal surface.

Cognitive Graph & Synaptic Fibres

The plastic recurrent neural network inside the membrane:

Directional Fibres (Synapses) Arrowheads show signal transmission. Cyan = excitatory, Coral = inhibitory, Amber = predictive/modulatory. Line thickness reflects synaptic weight.
Readout Anchors (Cyan Circles) Display live computed scalar activation values for motor or attention outputs.
Concept Nodes & Huber Loss Internal representations. Nodes experiencing high prediction error show a coral warning aura.

Beliefs & Contradiction Lines

Consolidated abstract beliefs (violet nodes) positioned by sensory affinity:

Node Radius & Opacity Radius scales with gathered empirical evidence (√evidence); opacity reflects Bayesian certainty.
Pulsing Coral Halo (Dissent) Preserved contradiction where evidence significantly disagreed with the baseline.
Dissent Path (Dashed Coral Arc) Tension line connecting the contested belief directly to the receptor channel that observed conflicting evidence.

Homeostatic HUD (Bottom)

Displays host acclimation progress and live sensory tier counts (Active / Probing / Dormant).

Reading the Population View

Visualizes collective relationships across up to 500 organisms in the fleet:

Ecology Mode Clusters organisms into elliptical envelopes by normalized environmental compatibility.
Knowledge Mode Links organisms by shared abstract patterns and retained belief volume.
Activity Mode Nodes sized and colored by cognitive activation and computational attention.
Dissent Mode Dashed coral lines isolate contested relationships and epistemic contradictions.
Comparative Inspection Click one organism (A), then another (B) to contrast memory, activity, and dissent in the sidebar.

Color Language

  • Cyan — Perception, sensory signals & excitatory synapses
  • Violet — Consolidated beliefs & latent concepts
  • Amber — Attention allocation, probing budget & recovery
  • Coral — Contradiction, prediction error & preserved dissent
  • Mint — Consent boundary, healthy homeostasis & utility

Passive Epistemic Boundary

The Observatory is strictly read-only: it ingests telemetry via loopback SSE streams or the local browser channel symbiont-observatory-v1. It never sends commands, controls, or alters the Symbiont.
