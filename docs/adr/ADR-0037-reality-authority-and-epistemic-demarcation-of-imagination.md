# ADR-0037: Reality Authority and Epistemic Demarcation of Imagination

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Generative cognition enables internal counterfactual simulation, trajectory construction, and hypothetical exploration of unobserved scenarios. However, deep generative models risk confabulation and epistemic drift: if internally generated dreams or hypothetical predictions are allowed to write directly to factual memory ledgers, the organism detaches from physical reality, treating hallucinated experiences as verified historical ground truth.

## Decision

1. **The Reality Authority Law.** Three constitutional axioms govern all generative and counterfactual operations:
   - *Reality may correct imagination:* Empirical sensory evidence always overrides internal model predictions;
   - *Imagination may propose reality:* Generative trajectories may formulate hypotheses, exploration goals, and candidate action proposals;
   - *Imagination must NEVER declare reality:* Counterfactual states are epistemically barred from being asserted as factual observations.
2. **Cognitive consolidation boundary.** Thinking and mental simulation may modify *how* the organism thinks (updating latent synaptic weights, predictive models, and heuristic priors), but thinking alone *cannot modify what the organism knows to be factual* (historical sensor logs and empirical evidence ledgers remain immutable).
3. **Endogenous cognitive targeting.** The organism autonomously selects what unresolved internal structures or surprise signals deserve counterfactual simulation. The laboratory apparatus must never dictate or force the targets of internal imagination.
4. **Metabolic bounding of imagination.** Non-factual generative simulation consumes finite metabolic energy. Hypotheses that remain unverified or fail to produce predictive utility must be excreted to free cognitive capacity.

## Consequences

- Prevents delusional cognitive divergence and hallucinations.
- Preserves the purity of empirical scientific evidence recorded in the organism's provenance graph.
- Balances the open-ended benefits of imaginative planning with rigorous empirical grounding.
