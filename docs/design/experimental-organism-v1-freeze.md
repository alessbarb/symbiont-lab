# Symbiont Experimental Organism v1 — freeze contract

## Scope

The current organism substrate is frozen by default after the final adversarial
readiness audit. This freezes the semantics and boundaries that make the
experimental subject reproducible; it does not freeze research, experiments,
analysis, Observatory, or ordinary maintenance.

## Frozen organism core

The frozen substrate includes the bounded organism runtime, physiology and
homeostasis, heredity and lifecycle, individual learning, per-organism Private
SLM state, provenance-preserving culture, autonomous bounded cultural agency,
opaque grounded symbols and structured communication, and their checkpoint /
replay contracts. The Integrated Habitat Runtime provides the canonical joint
population orchestration without adding a cognitive or linguistic policy.

## Allowed after freeze

- bug fixes and safety fixes;
- boundedness, checkpoint and reproducibility fixes;
- integration fixes that preserve existing semantics;
- experiment infrastructure and habitats;
- scientific analysis and negative-result preservation;
- passive Observatory and telemetry improvements;
- performance work that preserves causal semantics.

## Not allowed by default

- new cognition or learning capabilities;
- new social, cultural or language features;
- new inherited abilities, rewards or behavior policies;
- evaluator truth or semantic labels entering organism cognition;
- transfer of private models, weights or corpora;
- changes to immutable resource, security or host boundaries.

A proposed exception requires a new design/review gate and must not silently
modify the frozen contract.

> New phenomena should primarily be investigated through habitats and experiments, not by continuously adding organism capabilities.

Historical gates and negative results remain frozen and are not rewritten by
later observations.
