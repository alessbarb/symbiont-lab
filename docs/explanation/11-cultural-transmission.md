# Cultural Transmission

The capacity for organisms to communicate and share knowledge across a shared habitat represents one of the most profound evolutionary leaps within the Symbiont architecture. However, this process bears no resemblance to the direct, frictionless synchronization of databases or the raw streaming of internal telemetry. When an organism chooses to transmit a fragment of its experience, it encodes that knowledge into an entirely opaque structural artifact, releasing it into the communicative void. The receiving organism is then faced with an autonomous choice: to retain, mathematically validate, or completely ignore the incoming transmission, driven entirely by its own internal survival policies.

At the core of this exchange is a strict rejection of centralized semantics. Symbiont entities do not possess a hardcoded grammar, and the symbols they cast into the world have absolutely no intrinsic human meaning. When Organism A broadcasts a sequence of symbols, it is not transmitting absolute truth. For Organism B to find utility in the message, it must ground those opaque symbols entirely through the friction of its own independent sensory experience. The emergence of a shared convention between two organisms is never dictated from above; it is the organic result of independent agents discovering mutual, mathematical utility in the same sequence of symbols over time.

Because transmitted information is inherently unreliable, the receiving organism maintains a rigorous epistemological firewall. When a cultural artifact is intercepted, it is never immediately assimilated into the core belief structure. Instead, the organism routes the artifact into a dedicated `SocialEvidenceLedger`. This ledger is maintained in strict mathematical isolation from the organism's physical `CausalEvidenceLedger`, which tracks direct, first-person observations of the environment. 

This architectural separation is the organism's primary defense against the cognitive collapse of an echo chamber. Within the organism's mind, a copied claim remains explicitly marked as a copied claim—it can never masquerade as a direct observation and inflate the internal confidence counts falsely. If the organism subsequently experiences independent corroboration of the rumor, that new physical evidence remains distinguishable from the initial transmission. And if the environment violently contradicts the transmitted artifact, the structural contradiction is safely preserved, allowing the organism to slowly degrade its mathematical trust in the sender.

Yet, this system allows for the powerful mechanics of cumulative culture and compositional knowledge. An organism is not merely a passive receiver. It can take a socially acquired artifact, fuse it seamlessly with the empirical truths gathered in its own local evidence ledgers, and broadcast an entirely new, composite version back into the habitat. 

This act of cultural synthesis is meticulously tracked. The artifact's metadata computationally preserves the hashed lineage of contributor identity, allowing the network to trace exactly which organism appended what data. It retains the independent evidence roots, documenting the distinct, first-person physical experiences that ultimately support the composite claim. Finally, it records the exact version lineage, tracing the evolutionary path of the artifact itself. Because this rich, mathematical lineage is embedded deeply within the artifact's structure, a composite idea can survive, propagate, and remain highly useful long after its original founding organisms have ceased to exist, fully satisfying the prerequisites for genuine cumulative culture.

Ultimately, cultural transmission within Symbiont demonstrates that rich information can flow safely between autonomous agents without the need to open vulnerable network sockets or synchronize underlying source code. The entire phenomenon is driven by organism-side autonomy. The surrounding `SocialHabitat` merely provides the silent physical channel. It is the `OrganismRuntime.autonomous_social_step()` that mathematically weighs the value of the information against the strict constraints of the `MetabolicLedger`, deciding in total isolation what is worth sharing, and what is worth trusting.
