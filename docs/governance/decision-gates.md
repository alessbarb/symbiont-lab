# Owner decision gates

Stop before implementation or execution when any condition below is true.

## Scientific protocol

Owner approval is required to alter hypothesis, seed set, horizon, baseline, threshold, success criterion or interpretation rule; execute held-out or confirmation data; reopen a negative/frozen study as a new version; or convert an observational diagnostic into a production mechanism.

## Architecture

Owner approval is required to move responsibility between Symbiont, Lab and World; introduce a new organism capability; change lifecycle, death, identity, heredity, reproduction or re-embodiment semantics; or change the authority of private models, executive control or population orchestration.

## Project direction

Owner approval is required to reopen PAUSED, BLOCKED or UNSCHEDULED work; skip a roadmap phase/gate; resume population/cultural expansion before Individual Readiness; or change the canonical project priority.

## Host and safety

L4 approval plus ADR is required for new permission classes, identifying metadata or user-content access, arbitrary filesystem/process inspection, network scanning or autonomous exchange, credential access, hidden persistence, self-installation or propagation, privilege escalation, exploitation, stealth/evasion, autonomous real-world actuation/remediation, or learned control of hard resource ceilings.

## Epistemic boundary

L4 approval plus ADR is required to weaken evaluator-ground-truth isolation, no-global-reward policy, passive Observatory, opaque action/perception acquisition, or germline/acquired-state separation.

A new ADR cannot be inferred from code. It must be explicitly accepted before the implementation that depends on it.


## Scientific execution

Held-out, confirmation, replication and other evidentiary long runs require explicit
owner approval materialized as a `kind = "scientific-run"` grant. A code-change grant
does not authorize data consumption or campaign execution.
