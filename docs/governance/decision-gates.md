# Owner decision gates

Stop for an owner decision only when a change crosses one of these boundaries. Normal
engineering and new implementation inside already accepted invariants do not require a
separate permission ceremony.

## Scientific protocol

Owner decision is required to alter a preregistered hypothesis, seed set, horizon,
baseline, threshold, success criterion or interpretation rule; consume held-out,
confirmation or replication data; reopen a negative/frozen study as a new version; or
turn an observational diagnostic into a production mechanism.

## Architecture

Owner decision is required when responsibility moves between Symbiont, Lab and World,
or when a change alters a protected constitutional invariant such as lifecycle,
identity, heredity, reproduction, re-embodiment semantics, evaluator isolation or the
authority of private models/executive control.

Adding a capability inside already accepted boundaries is not by itself an owner gate.

## Project direction

Owner decision is required to reopen PAUSED, BLOCKED or UNSCHEDULED work, skip a
canonical roadmap gate, resume population/cultural expansion before Individual
Readiness, or change canonical project priority.

## Host and safety

An Accepted ADR plus explicit owner decision is required for new permission classes,
identifying metadata or user-content access, arbitrary filesystem/process inspection,
network scanning or autonomous exchange, credential access, hidden persistence,
self-installation or propagation, privilege escalation, exploitation, stealth/evasion,
autonomous real-world actuation/remediation, or learned control of hard resource ceilings.

## Epistemic boundary

An Accepted ADR plus explicit owner decision is required to weaken evaluator-ground-
truth isolation, no-global-reward policy, passive Observatory, opaque action/perception
acquisition, or germline/acquired-state separation.

## Scientific execution

Held-out, confirmation and replication runs require an explicit owner decision before
launch. This is a policy boundary, not a repository grant. `run start` pins code and
input state, enforces resource/concurrency limits and writes the execution receipt; it
does not claim to authenticate the human decision.
